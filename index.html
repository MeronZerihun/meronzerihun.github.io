Research

Current

Reliable memory for AI training

Large-scale AI training keeps a lot of state in GPU memory, and memory errors are a common reason training jobs stop. I study which corruptions of that state actually harm a model, and how hardware can use what it knows about the workload to protect training more efficiently.

Earlier

Hardware for privacy and integrity

Hardware that lets the cloud compute on sensitive data without exposing it, and that gives users verifiable evidence that a remote computation ran correctly, including safe, selective disclosure of results.
